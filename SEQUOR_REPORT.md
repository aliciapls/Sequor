# Sequor — AI Communication Coverage Platform

**Repository:** https://github.com/aliciapls/Sequor

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Sequor is an AI-powered communication coverage platform for small businesses. When the owner is unavailable, Sequor handles incoming email and WhatsApp messages — classifying them, generating auto-replies from uploaded documents, and escalating only what needs human attention.

**How it works:** Customer messages → AI classifies (routine / semi-routine / complex / high-stakes) → High-confidence routine queries get an immediate auto-reply drawn from uploaded documents → Complex or low-confidence queries escalate to a backup contact with an AI-prepared draft ready for review → Every human resolution improves future auto-replies.

**Channels:** Email (SendGrid) and WhatsApp Business API (Meta Cloud API).

**Pricing:** Free (50 messages/month) → Starter SGD 20/month (200 messages) → Professional → Enterprise. Paid plans via Stripe.

**Compliance:** PDPA (Singapore) — consent tracking, right to erasure, HUMAN override, PII encrypted at rest.

**ROI Snapshot:** A Singapore SME spending 10 hours/month on routine email replies at SGD 25/hr saves SGD 250/month in staff time, plus an estimated SGD 7,500/month in recovered lost deals. Annual cost with Sequor: SGD 600. Annual cost without: SGD 93,000.

## 2. Business Case

### The Problem

Small businesses lose customers when they cannot respond quickly. Hiring a virtual assistant costs SGD 1,500–3,000/month. Sequor provides always-on AI coverage at a fraction of that cost.

| ------------------- | --------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Monthly cost        | From free | SGD 1,500–3,000   | SGD 80–500+ | SGD 50–500+ | SGD 0       |
| Setup time          | Hours     | Days to weeks     | Days        | Days        | N/A         |
| Escalation+AI draft | Yes       | N/A               | Partial     | No          | No          |
| PDPA built-in       | Yes       | No                | Partial     | No          | No          |

### What Is Needed to Go Live

1. **DNS verification** — MX/TXT records generated by Sequor. Takes 5–30 minutes.
2. **WhatsApp Business Account** (if using WhatsApp) — Meta Business Account, verify phone number, approve Cloud API app. Takes 1–2 hours.
3. **SendGrid account** (if using email) — Free tier sufficient for most.
4. **Upload initial documents** — FAQ and policy documents minimum.
5. **Set escalation contacts** — At least one human backup contact.
6. **Configure SLA** — Hours before an unresolved query escalates.

Total setup time for a non-technical owner: **2–4 hours**.

### Data Continuity

If Sequor ceases operation: all data exported in CSV/JSON on request; database self-hosted via Docker image; no proprietary lock-in.

## 3. User Guide — Day-to-Day Operations

### What You Will Receive as a Backup Contact

When an escalation is created, you receive this email:

```
Subject: [UNRESOLVED] Question about bulk order pricing (Ref: a1b2c3d4)

Client: Sarah Tan (WhatsApp) · Received: 14 May 2026, 9:42 AM
Confidence: Moderate (67%) — semi_routine

Hi, do you offer discounts for bulk orders of 500+ units?

→ Reply to this email to send your response to the client.
→ If unresolved by 14 May 2026, 2:00 PM, this escalates to John Lim.

AI suggested response:
Yes, we offer bulk discounts. Orders of 500+ units qualify for our
wholesale rate, 15% below standard pricing.
```

Your options: reply directly, edit the AI draft, or compose your own. The draft is a starting point, not a constraint.

### HUMAN Override

Customers type **HUMAN** (or HUMAN help) in any message to force immediate escalation. Their contact record is flagged so your team handles them manually going forward.

### Common Tasks

- **Upload a document:** Documents → Upload → PDF/DOCX/TXT (max 25MB) → assign type → wait for Ready status.
- **Add a key phrase:** Key Phrases → Add → phrase → assign to document → set confidence boost. Use Suggest for AI-proposed phrases.
- **Handle a PDPA erasure request:** Settings → Compliance → Erase Contact Data → enter email. All personal data deleted within 30 days.
- **SLA breach:** Each account has a configurable SLA (default: 4 hours). Unresolved escalations automatically escalate to second-tier contact with a breach notification.

### Troubleshooting

| Situation                                        | Likely Cause                    | Action                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Customer says they replied; you didn't receive   | Message escalated               | Check Escalations list                |
| Auto-reply sounds wrong                          | Document outdated or wrong type | Re-upload document, check type        |
| Escalation email not received                    | Backup email wrong or spam      | Check Contacts → backup email         |
| WhatsApp got template instead of free-form reply | 24-hour session expired         | No action needed; working as designed |
| Document stuck at "Indexing"                     | Restart mid-processing          | Corrects on next startup              |

### Glossary

| Term                               | Meaning                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Pending / Indexing / Ready / Stale | Document processing statuses                   |
| Key Phrase                         | Keyword linking a query to a specific document |
| Auto-reply                         | AI-generated message sent automatically        |
| Escalate                           | Route to human backup contact                  |
| PDPA                               | Singapore Personal Data Protection Act         |
| Human Override                     | Contact flagged for always-manual handling     |

## 4. Technical Overview

### Architecture

```
Customer (Email/WhatsApp) → SendGrid / Meta Webhooks → FastAPI on Vercel
  |-- InboundEmailProcessor / InboundWhatsAppProcessor
  |-- AutoReplyService
  |     |-- MessageClassifier (LLM — Ollama or OpenAI)
  |     |-- RAGPipeline (pgvector similarity + BM25 keyword)
  |     |-- ResponseGenerator
  |     |-- LearningLoop
  |-- EscalationService
  |-- BillingService (Stripe)
         |-- PostgreSQL + pgvector
```

**AI:** Ollama `llama3.1` + `nomic-embed-text` locally; OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small` as fallback. Without embeddings, BM25 keyword search handles retrieval. OpenAI costs ~USD $0.02 per 1M tokens.

### Response Routing

| Confidence | Category                | Action                 |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| >= 90%     | routine/semi_routine    | Auto-reply sent        |
| 60–90%     | routine/semi_routine    | Escalate with AI draft |
| < 60%      | any                     | Escalate without draft |
| any        | high_stakes or critical | Immediate escalation   |

**Learning loop:** Human escalation resolutions captured as Q&A pairs and embedded. Future queries search this learned set alongside documents.

### Key API Endpoints

| Endpoint                                  | Method | Auth | Description                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------ | ---- | -------------------------------------- |
| `/api/v1/onboarding`                      | POST   | No   | Create tenant, account, backup contact |
| `/api/v1/auth/login`                      | POST   | No   | Login → JWT cookie                     |
| `/api/v1/portal/documents/upload`         | POST   | Yes  | Upload document                        |
| `/api/v1/portal/documents/{id}`           | DELETE | Yes  | Delete document                        |
| `/api/v1/portal/escalations`              | GET    | Yes  | List escalations                       |
| `/api/v1/portal/escalations/{id}/resolve` | POST   | Yes  | Resolve escalation                     |
| `/api/v1/email/inbound`                   | POST   | No   | SendGrid webhook                       |
| `/api/v1/whatsapp/inbound`                | POST   | No   | Meta webhook                           |
| `/api/v1/billing/webhook`                 | POST   | No   | Stripe webhook                         |
| `/api/v1/portal/upgrade`                  | POST   | Yes  | Stripe checkout                        |

### Environment Variables (Required)

| Variable                | Description                                         |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `DATABASE_URL`          | PostgreSQL connection string                        |
| `JWT_SECRET`            | JWT signing secret (min 32 chars)                   |
| `ENCRYPTION_MASTER_KEY` | Base64 32-byte key for PII encryption               |
| `APP_ENV`               | `production` on Vercel                              |
| `OPENAI_API_KEY`        | Enables semantic embeddings (optional; costs apply) |
| `SENDGRID_API_KEY`      | For email                                           |
| `STRIPE_API_KEY`        | For billing                                         |
| `WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN` | For WhatsApp                                        |

PostgreSQL + pgvector is required — managed instances from ~SGD 5/month. SQLite cannot support hybrid RAG (70% vector + 30% BM25).

### WhatsApp Constraints

Meta's 24-hour session window: free-form replies only within 24 hours of a customer message. Pre-approved template messages required outside the window. HUMAN triggers forced escalation regardless of confidence.

1. `src/sequor/onboarding/app.py` — All routes and handlers

### Local Setup

```bash
uv venv && uv sync
cp .env.example .env  # fill in values
python -c "CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS vector;"  # in PostgreSQL
uv run uvicorn sequor.onboarding.app:app --reload
```

### Docker (Self-Hosting)

```bash
docker build -t sequor .
docker run -d --name sequor -p 8080:8080 \
  -e DATABASE_URL="postgresql://..." \
  -e JWT_SECRET="your-32-char-secret" \
  -e ENCRYPTION_MASTER_KEY="base64-key" \
  -e APP_ENV="production" \
  -e SENDGRID_API_KEY="SG.xxx" \
  -e STRIPE_API_KEY="sk_live_xxx" \
  -e WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN="EAAxxx" \
  sequor
```

### Debugging

| Problem                     | Check                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Documents stuck at Indexing | `UPDATE documents SET status='ready' WHERE status='indexing';`             |
| Auto-replies not sending    | Ollama running (`curl localhost:11434/api/tags`); `OPENAI_API_KEY` set     |

## 6. Pre-Launch Checklist

All critical and high items must be resolved before production traffic.

### Critical — Must Fix Before Any User Traffic

| #   | Gap                                                                                                                         | Fix                                                      |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **SLA scheduler never starts** — `create_scheduler()` never called. Breach detection and second-tier escalation never fire. | Add scheduler to FastAPI lifespan in `onboarding/app.py` |
| 2   | **JWT dev-secret fallback** — unset `JWT_SECRET` falls back to `"dev-secret-change-in-production"`. Tokens can be forged.   | Remove fallback in `auth.py:54-64`; raise on startup     |
| 3   | **PDPA erasure leaves Message.body_text** — customer message body remains after erasure request.                            | Add `body_text` nullification in `compliance.py:133-140` |
| 4   | **Plan limits not enforced** — free tier 50-message limit is display-only.                                                  | Add limit check in email/WhatsApp inbound handlers       |

### High — Should Fix Before Launch

| #   | Gap                                                                                                              | Fix                                                              |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5   | WhatsApp template not verified — `"acknowledgement"` hardcoded; silent failure if not approved in Meta.          | Add template existence check in `whatsapp/auto_reply.py:349-351` |
| 6   | No email ChannelConsent — WhatsApp tracks consent; email does not. PDPA gap.                                     | Add `_ensure_consent()` in `email/inbound.py`                    |
| 7   | Chunk search loads all rows to memory — `VectorStore.search()` fetches all then filters in Python. OOM at scale. | Add SQL LIMIT in `vector_store.py:122-131`                       |
| 8   | No health check endpoint — load balancers have no `/health`.                                                     | Add `@app.get("/health")` in `onboarding/app.py`                 |
| 9   | Over-broad PDPA erasure — no messages → deletes ALL tenant chunk embeddings.                                     | Scope deletion to contact's own data in `compliance.py:147-149`  |

### Medium — Address Before Scaling

- WhatsApp escalation resolutions don't feed learning loop (only email does)
- Hallucination check silently passes on malformed LLM output
- Rate limiter per-worker (in-memory), not shared across uvicorn workers
- Stripe event deduplication per-worker, not distributed-safe
- No `/metrics` observability endpoint

## 7. Honest Assessment — Product Weak Points

| Weak Point                                                       | Business Impact                                 | Mitigation                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Learning loop cold start — no escalation history for new tenants | First weeks rely entirely on uploaded documents | Invest in high-quality documents before going live           |
| WhatsApp template approval takes 24–72 hours                     | No WhatsApp auto-replies until Meta approves    | Start Meta verification early in onboarding                  |
| Document quality sets the ceiling                                | AI accuracy limited by document coverage        | Review and update documents regularly                        |
| No uptime SLA on Free/Starter                                    | No guarantee of availability                    | Self-host with Docker for production SLA needs               |
| Vercel free tier 10s serverless timeout                          | Documents >10MB may fail mid-processing         | Self-host for large volumes; keep files under 10MB on Vercel |
| Escalation replies require human confirmation                    | Complex queries always need a human in the loop | Use HUMAN override for fully automated routine queries       |

## 8. Conclusion

Sequor addresses a real problem: small businesses losing customers to slow response times, at a cost that is a fraction of a virtual assistant. The core AI pipeline — classification, hybrid RAG retrieval, confidence-based routing, and the learning loop — is a solid foundation. Email and WhatsApp are handled with a coherent escalation workflow that reduces operator burden rather than adding noise.

The pre-launch checklist identifies 15 gaps. Four are critical: the SLA scheduler not running, the JWT dev-secret fallback, incomplete PDPA erasure, and unenforced plan limits. None require architectural changes. The codebase audit confirmed the core AI logic is implemented, not stubbed.

Sequor is ready for a controlled pilot with 1–3 early-adopter customers. It is not ready for mass market without resolving the four critical pre-launch items.
