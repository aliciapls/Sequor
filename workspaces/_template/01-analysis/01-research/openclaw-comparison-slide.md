## What Is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an **open-source personal AI assistant** that runs on your own machine.

- Connects to WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack, iMessage
- Reads your email, sends messages, manages your calendar
- You need to install, configure, and maintain it
- You provide your own AI model API keys
- Free to use — but requires technical knowledge to operate

**Best for:** Developers and tech enthusiasts who want a personal AI assistant running locally on their own hardware.

---

## What Is Sequor?

Sequor is a **team coverage layer** for businesses.

- Covers WhatsApp and email — no installation needed
- AI reads, classifies, resolves routine queries, routes everything else
- RAG on your internal documents (FAQs, price lists, policies)
- Escalates to backup contacts when needed — with SLA tracking
- PDPA-compliant by design
- Works for solo operators, 3-person firms, and enterprises

**Best for:** Any team — especially professional services firms — that cannot afford to miss client messages when someone is OOO or in a meeting.

## Use Case Comparison

### Scenario: The office manager goes on leave for a week

**OpenClaw:**

- OpenClaw can be configured to auto-reply to messages
- Only works while your computer is on
- No backup contact if it fails
- No routing to a team member
- No record of what was handled

**Sequor:**

- Configure OOO window in 2 minutes
- AI handles routine queries from your documents
- Complex queries route to a backup colleague
- Full audit trail of every routing decision
- SLA monitoring if backup doesn't respond
