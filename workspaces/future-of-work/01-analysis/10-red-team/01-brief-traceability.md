- **Location:** `specs/platform-overview.md` §4 (seven "→ _Detailed sibling spec:_" pointers); `specs/connectors-and-integration.md` §7 "It does NOT cover:" block + inline `specs/...` citations.
- **What's wrong (grep/ls-verified — every one of these files is absent from `specs/`):**

  - `connectors-and-integration.md` §7 declares "It does NOT cover: `specs/orchestration-runtime.md` ... `specs/governance-substrate.md` ... `specs/provenance-and-versioning.md` ... `specs/artifact-system.md`" — all four phantom — and the body cites `specs/governance-substrate.md` (×5), `specs/provenance-and-versioning.md` (×3), `specs/record-model.md` (×2) as if authoritative.

- **Location:** `specs/platform-overview.md` §4 (L7 → `work-interface.md`; L2 → `orchestration-runtime.md`); `specs/_index.md` (platform table lists 7 specs but only 5 moat specs + overview + connectors exist — L7 and L2 are absent).
