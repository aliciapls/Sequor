# 0007 — TRADE-OFF: lead the story with M4 (the moat) but ship M2 (governance) first

- **Type:** TRADE-OFF
- **Date:** 2026-07-03
- **Phase:** /todos → /codify (workspace: future-of-work)
- **Author:** jack-hong

## The competing approaches

**Option A (rejected) — all-in on the M1/C5 cascade engine first.** Lead AND ship the headline "undo-anything" moat before governance. Rejected: it stakes the entire competitive position on winning a race for the _hardest-to-build_ term against better-resourced competitors; a lost race leaves nothing else far enough along to fall back on (analysis 03 §8.5; 09 §5.3).

**Option B (chosen) — ship M2 (governance) first; lead the _story_ with M4.** Governance (C2) is the most-proven, lowest-risk, regulation-backed moat AND it produces the posture-stamped step log the cascade engine (C5) reads. So the headline is built on running, trusted ground; the platform is credible at every stage even if C5 takes longer.

## Why B

- **Decouples differentiation from execution risk** — the narrative leads with the strongest moat (M4/undo), the build leads with the safest (M2/governance).
- **The conjunction survives losing one term** — if C5's legibility bet fails, the platform still owns a unique agnostic + natively-governed + governed-cross-org combination no suite vendor or horizontal harness matches.
- **Cost of B (honest):** two moats build in the right order = more coordination than a single sprint; justified because the ~80% reuse carries most of the load.
