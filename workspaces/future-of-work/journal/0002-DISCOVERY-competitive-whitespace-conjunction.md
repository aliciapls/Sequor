# 0002 — DISCOVERY: the defensible whitespace is the CONJUNCTION (M1+M2+M3+M4), not any single property

- **Type:** DISCOVERY
- **Date:** 2026-06-06
- **Phase:** /analyze → /codify (workspace: future-of-work)
- **Author:** jack-hong

## Finding

Competitive research (`01-analysis/01-research/07-competitive-landscape.md`) found the vision lands in a crowded macro-category but a sparse intersection: almost every individual claim is asserted by someone, few are delivered well, and **nobody delivers the full combination in one agnostic, non-coder interface.**

- **M1** (versioned, intervene-from-any-step) exists only as a _developer_ primitive (LangGraph time-travel / durable execution) — no non-coder product does it.
- **M3**'s _human-multiplicity_ half is rare (A2A commoditizes agent↔agent).
- **M4** governed cross-org artifact exchange is open at the _trust/provenance_ layer (marketplaces are publish/consume directories).
- **Claude Cowork** (GA Apr 2026) owns the _surface_ ("agent does your work in one interface") and ships ~fortnightly.

## Why it matters

The platform must win on the **substrate** (M1–M4), never the surface. Suite vendors (ServiceNow/Salesforce/SAP/Microsoft) are vertical _by business model_ → that is the agnosticism whitespace. The single most-differentiated bet is the M1 + M3(human) + M4 conjunction.
