Setup Guide

Connect your channels and get started

WhatsApp
1Create a Meta Business Account — free.
2In Meta Business, go to Connections → WhatsApp. Create an app and add your phone number.
3Copy your Phone Number ID and Business Account ID from the Meta developer portal.
4Generate a permanent access token in the Meta developer portal.
5Go to Channels and enter those three values.
Email
1Create a free SendGrid account.
2In SendGrid, go to Settings → Sender Authentication → Domain Authentication. Enter a subdomain (e.g. mail) and your domain. Add the DNS records SendGrid gives you at your domain registrar.
3In SendGrid, go to Settings → Inbound Parse. Add your authenticated domain and set the URL to the endpoint shown on your Channels page.
4Go to Channels and enter your domain (e.g. mail.yourdomain.com).
Documents
1Go to Document Hub and upload your PDF, DOCX, or TXT files. These teach the AI what to respond with.
2Go to Key Phrase Maps and link common phrases customers use to your documents.
Glossary
Pending
Your document was saved but hasn't started processing yet. It will move to Indexing shortly.
Indexing
The document is being processed — parsed, chunked, and converted into vector embeddings by the AI. Once complete it moves to Ready.
Ready
The document is fully processed and the AI can use it to answer client queries.
Key Phrase
A word or phrase (e.g. "pricing", "refund policy") that triggers Sequor to retrieve a specific document when a client mentions it.
Key Phrase Mapping
The link between a key phrase and a document. When a client message contains that phrase, Sequor retrieves the mapped document to build its reply.
Document Type
Categorizes your document to help Sequor use it appropriately — FAQ (help articles), Policy, Price List, Roster (directory), or Other.
Auto-reply
A mapping type where Sequor sends a full AI-generated answer drawn from the mapped document, without human review.
Escalate
A mapping type where Sequor flags the message for human review instead of auto-replying.
Include in Context
A mapping type where Sequor attaches the document to the AI's context but writes its own reply rather than quoting the document directly.
