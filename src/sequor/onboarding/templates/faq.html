Help & Setup Guide

Get your account running in minutes

Connect WhatsApp
You need: A Meta Business Account and a phone number registered with WhatsApp Business.
1
Create a Meta Business Account
Go to business.meta.com and sign up. This is free. Follow the steps to verify your business.
2
Create a WhatsApp Business App
In your Meta Business Account, go to Connections → WhatsApp and create a WhatsApp Business app. Add the phone number you want to use — it must be on WhatsApp already.
3
Copy your credentials
In your WhatsApp Business app, find the Phone Number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID. You'll need both.
4
Add a permanent access token
In your Meta developer portal, generate a permanent access token for the app. Copy it too. Keep it private.
5
Enter credentials in Sequor
Go to Channels, enter your Phone Number ID, Business Account ID, and Access Token. Click Save.
Connect Email
You need: A domain you control (e.g. yourdomain.com) and a SendGrid account (free tier works).
1
Create a SendGrid account
Go to sendgrid.com and sign up for free. Verify your sender identity (your email address).
2
Create a dedicated email domain
In SendGrid, go to Settings → Sender Authentication → Domain Authentication. Enter a subdomain (e.g. mail) and your root domain. SendGrid will give you DNS records to add.
3
Add DNS records at your domain registrar
Add the CNAME and MX records SendGrid provides to your domain's DNS settings (where you bought the domain). This lets SendGrid receive emails on your behalf.
4
Set up inbound parsing
In SendGrid, go to Settings → Inbound Parse → Add Host & URL. Set the domain to the domain you authenticated. Set the URL to the inbound endpoint shown on your Channels page.
5
Enter your domain in Sequor
Go to Channels, enter the domain you authenticated (e.g. mail.yourdomain.com) and click Save.
Add Your Documents
1
Go to Document Hub
Click Document Hub in the sidebar. This is where Sequor stores your knowledge base for AI responses.
2
Upload your files
Upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT files. These are used by the AI to generate accurate auto-replies based on your company's policies and information.
3
Add key phrase maps
Go to Key Phrase Maps to link common customer phrases to your documents. This helps the AI find the right answer.
