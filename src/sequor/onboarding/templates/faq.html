Help & Setup Guide

Get your account running in minutes

Connect WhatsApp
You need: A Meta Business Account and a phone number on WhatsApp Business.
1
Create a Meta Business Account at business.meta.com — it's free.
2
In your Meta Business Account, go to Connections → WhatsApp and create a WhatsApp Business app. Add your phone number — it must already be on WhatsApp.
3
Copy your Phone Number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID from the Meta developer portal.
4
Generate a permanent access token in the Meta developer portal for your app.
5
Go to Channels and enter your Phone Number ID, Business Account ID, and Access Token.
Connect Email
You need: A domain you control and a free SendGrid account.
1
Create a free account at sendgrid.com. Verify your sender identity (your email).
2
In SendGrid, go to Settings → Sender Authentication → Domain Authentication. Enter a subdomain (e.g. mail) and your root domain. Add the DNS records SendGrid gives you.
3
Add the CNAME and MX records at your domain registrar (where you bought the domain).
4
In SendGrid, go to Settings → Inbound Parse → Add Host & URL. Set the domain to your authenticated domain. Set the URL to the endpoint shown on your Channels page.
5
Go to Channels and enter your domain (e.g. mail.yourdomain.com).
Add Your Documents
1
Go to Document Hub in the sidebar. Upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT files. These become your AI knowledge base.
2
Go to Key Phrase Maps to link common customer phrases to your documents. This helps the AI find the right answers.
